## **XP sources**

| XP source | Prize | Frequency | Exact implementation rule |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Verify email and activate account | **50 XP** | Once | Award after the verification link or code is successfully confirmed. |
| Add and verify the phone number | **25 XP** | Once | Award only after the phone number passes any required verification. Do not award for repeatedly replacing the method. |
| First authenticated login or check-in of the day | **10 XP** | Once per day | Multiple sessions, browsers or devices do not generate additional XP. |
| Reach a streak milestone | **Variable** | Once per milestone | Reward sustained MyVoice log-in at 3 days 30 XP, 7 days 100 XP, 14 days 250XP, 30 days 1000XP, 60 days 3000XP, 100 days 10000XP and 365 days 50000XP. |
| Complete the first profile section | **50 XP** | Once | Award when the first full profiling section reaches a valid completed state. |
| Complete each additional profile section | **25 XP** | Once per section | Each section has its own completion event. Re-saving the same answers does not count. |
| Reach 50% profile completion | **50 XP** | Once | A milestone reward added on top of the underlying section-completion XP. |
| Reach 100% profile completion | **100 XP** | Once | Award only when all currently mandatory profiling sections are complete. |
| Complete a profile with new questions | **25 XP** | Once | Used when MyVoice adds new profiling questions after the member originally reached 100%. |
| Complete a newly introduced mandatory profile module | **50 XP** | Once per module | Used when MyVoice adds a genuinely new profiling area after the member originally reached 100%. |
| Start a survey successfully | **10 XP** | Once per unique survey | After the member is taken to the survey page. |
| Receive an eligible screenout | **25 XP** | Once per unique survey | Award only after the survey system returns a valid screenout status. |
| Receive an eligible quota-full outcome | **25 XP** | Once per unique survey | Award only after a valid quota-full callback. |
| Reconcile to Complete | **25 XP** | Once per unique survey | Award only when the reconciled to a complete. |
| Complete the first valid survey on MyVoice | **100 XP** | Once | An early milestone designed to bring a new member close to their next level. |
| Complete a valid survey | **Variable** | Every confirmed completion | Base XP should be linked to expected survey effort and awarded only after the completion is accepted. Recommended survey-completion formula I recommend calculating base completion XP from the survey’s estimated length rather than its monetary reward: Base completion XP \= 25 \+ (5 × estimated survey minutes) Examples: Expected survey length Base completion XP 5 minutes 50 XP 10 minutes 75 XP 15 minutes 100 XP 20 minutes 125 XP 30 minutes 175 XP |
| Resume a dropout survey and successfully completed it | **10 XP** | Once per unique survey | The system must detect a genuine earlier session and a later successful completion. Refreshing the page does not qualify. |
| Reach a valid survey-completion milestone | **Variable** | Once per milestone | Suggested milestones: 5 surveys 50 XP, 10 surveys 100XP, 15 surveys 150 XP, 20 surveys 200 XP, 25 surveys 250 XP and so on. |
| Complete the first successful reward redemption | **100 XP** | Once | Award when the first payout is successfully processed, and the status is updated by the Incentives colleague. |
